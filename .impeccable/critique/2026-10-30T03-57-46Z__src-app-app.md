## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The interface is coherent and feels like a deliberately assembled admin product. Shared navigation, restrained borders, card radius, table density, and semantic status colors work together. It is somewhat conventional for an admin dashboard, but consistency is strong and avoids arbitrary page-by-page styling.

**Deterministic scan:** 94 route files were scanned. Two signals: `examples/components/file-upload/page.tsx:69` is likely a false positive because the image source is assigned from a user-selected file at runtime; `examples/components/spinners/page.tsx:9` uses `animate-bounce`, likely intentional in a spinner demo.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay was produced; the fallback was direct browser screenshots and detector output.

## Overall Impression

The sampled pages look like one application: the shell and common data components are consistent on desktop and at 390px mobile width. The most disruptive inconsistency is inside Dashboard itself: Revenue Overview and Traffic Sources have blank chart areas, while the surrounding labels imply that data should be visible.

## What's Working

- Sidebar hierarchy and active navigation treatment remain consistent across dashboard, catalog, activity, settings, calendar, and order flows.
- Products and Orders use compatible table, search, filter, badge, and card patterns, so switching between them does not require relearning the layout.
- Typography is restrained in the sampled routes: page titles are consistently around 24px, with compact controls and clear text hierarchy. Discounts promo codes are now 12px monospace, reducing their visual dominance.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Dashboard charts render as blank areas.** The Revenue Overview plot and Traffic Sources donut are not visible, though the chart panels and legends are present. This hides the comparisons the dashboard is meant to provide and makes the page look unfinished. Verify the chart container has a non-zero measured size after layout and that client rendering/data reach the chart; then confirm both plots render at desktop and mobile widths. Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`.
- **[P2] Confirm tablet-width app-shell overflow.** Discounts measured a document width of 802px at a 768px viewport, while representative routes checked at desktop and 390px mobile did not overflow. This may be a shared topbar/sidebar constraint, but it was not confirmed across routes. Check the shared shell at 768px before changing individual page layouts. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P3] Dashboard vertical density pushes secondary panels below the first screen.** Recent Orders and Top Products are lower on the page. This is acceptable for a scrollable admin dashboard, but users scanning quickly may miss them. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (First-Timer):** Icon-led sidebar items and actions may need tooltips or accessible labels. The sampled pages use recognizable labels, but contextual help is sparse.
- **Sam (Data-Focused Operator):** On Dashboard, the empty Revenue Overview and Traffic Sources plots prevent quick trend and source comparison even though the legends suggest data exists.
